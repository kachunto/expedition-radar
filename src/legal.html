Privacy

What the app stores on your device. Your progress (which items you ticked, your story point, your settings) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is not sent to us. "Reset progress" deletes it.

Statistics. FILL_IN describe the analytics you enabled, for example: "We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without tracking you across sites." Delete this paragraph if you use none.

Problem reports. The "Report a problem" links open a form on GitHub. Whatever you submit there is handled by GitHub and is public. The link includes only the item's name and id, nothing about you.
